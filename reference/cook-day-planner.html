Pit Boss 820 notes

  • 849 sq in total: about 593 main grate + 256 upper rack. 180-500F range.
  • Hopper is 21 lb on the Sportsman and 820D3; the Pro Series 820 is 19 lb. Set yours above.
  • Pellet grills commonly swing 30-40F around set point. Verify at grate level with a probe.
  • Climbing from 225F to 400F takes roughly 10-20 min depending on weather; the planner budgets 15.
  • Pellet burn rates above are estimates. Cold, wind and lid opening push them up.
  • Wings on a wire rack for airflow; ribs in a rib rack to save main-grate space.

Food safety minimums (USDA FSIS)

  • All poultry, including breasts and wings: 165F.
  • Whole-muscle beef and pork (steaks, roasts, chops): 145F plus a 3 min rest.
  • Brisket, pork butt and rib targets (195-205F) are for collagen breakdown and texture, not safety.
  • Medium-rare tri-tip (130-135F) is below the USDA 145F minimum: your call.
  • Hot holding: keep it at 140F or above (FDA Food Code uses 135F).

Holding

  • Brisket and pork butt hold well for 2-4 hr wrapped in a towel-lined cooler, or in the oven at the lowest setting that stays above 140F.
  • Ribs hold about 1 hr wrapped in foil before the bark softens.
  • Wings hold uncovered only, 20-30 min max, or the skin goes rubbery.
  • Chicken breasts hold poorly. Time them to come off right before serving.
  • Have the oven at hold temp before the first protein comes off.
