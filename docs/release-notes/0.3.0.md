For people updating from 0.2.0. Install it over the old version: do not uninstall first.
How to install or update: [INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md).

## A new look

The app was rebuilt from the ground up. It does the same job on the same three tabs (Plan, Cook, Gear),
but every screen is new. Text follows your phone's font size, and times and temperatures are never cut
off.

## Your data comes with you

The first time you open 0.3.0, it brings your data over from 0.2.0: your smokers and other cookers, your
cook rates, the plan you were working on, and your cook history. A cook that is still running comes over
too, with its alerts.

You see a notice once, titled "Pit Planner has a new look", with one of these lines:

- "Your smokers, cook rates, plan and history came over." Everything moved.
- "Some of your old settings couldn't be read. Check Gear before your next cook." Open Gear and check your
  cookers.
- "Your old data couldn't be brought over. Set up your smoker in Gear to start."

Tap OK to close it.

## What changed

Plan

- Two of the same food are told apart. With two briskets on the menu, the cards, steps and alerts say
  "11.5 lb brisket" and "6 lb brisket".
- Add food opens the food's details first. It goes on the menu when you tap Add to plan.
- The Conflicts fixes are: move to griddle, change temp, or edit the food. "Finish earlier" is gone.
- In Celsius, smoker temps are offered in 2C steps.
- If sharing a plan fails, the app says "Couldn't share".

Cook

- Only Start cook on Plan starts a cook. With no cook running, the Cook tab says "No cook running". While
  a cook is running, Start cook is greyed out, so a running cook is never replaced by accident.
- Start over asks first ("Start over?") and never touches your cook history.
- Next up reads as one sentence with the time left, for example "Check the brisket in 1 hr 12 min".
- If the phone refuses to set the cook's alarms, the Cook screen says "Alarms could not be set. Steps will
  not ring." with Try again.
- Quiet alerts for 30 min now also holds back the repeat alerts.
- A cook in progress survives a font size change and the phone closing the app in the background.
- If the phone restarts during a cook, the alarms come back by themselves. Unlock the phone once after a
  restart: on some phones (seen on a OnePlus) no alert sounds until the first unlock.

Gear

- The Alerts sheet has "Send a test alert in 10 sec", so you can check alerts any time.
- Cook history: a past cook can be edited or removed, and left out of your cook rates.
- If your smoker's temps go up in different steps than the app shows, pick "Not listed? Enter it yourself" and
  set your own step.
