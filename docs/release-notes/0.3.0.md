# Pit Planner 0.3.0

For people updating from 0.2.0. Install it over the old version: do not uninstall first.
How to install or update: [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/MechEng70/pitplanner/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).

## A new look

The app was rebuilt from the ground up. It does the same job on the same three tabs (Plan, Cook, Gear),
but every screen is new. Text follows your phone's font size, and times and temperatures are never cut
off. Buttons in sheets stay on the screen at larger font sizes, including Replace in "Replace your data?",
Start over in "Start over?" and the buttons in "Alerts may not ring".

## Your data comes with you

The first time you open 0.3.0, it brings your data over from 0.2.0: your smokers and other cookers, your
cook rates, the plan you were working on, and your cook history. A cook that is still running comes over
too, with its alerts.

You see a notice once, titled "Pit Planner has a new look", with one of these lines:

- "Your smokers, cook rates, plan and history came over." Everything moved.
- "Some of your old settings couldn't be read. Check Gear before your next cook." Open Gear and check your
  cookers.
- "Your old data couldn't be brought over. Set up your smoker in Gear to start."

If your oven hold in 0.2.0 was below 140F, the notice also says "Oven hold raised to 140F, the safe
minimum." Tap OK to close it.

## What changed

Plan

- Two of the same food are told apart. With two briskets on the menu, the cards, steps and alerts say
  "11.5 lb brisket" and "6 lb brisket".
- Whole turkey is a new food, cooked on the smoker. The plan uses 165F as the done temp, in the thigh, the
  wing and the thickest part of the breast, and rests it 30 min. Above 16 lb the plan adds a note:
  "Whole turkey is 16.5 lb. USDA recommends 16 lb or less for safe smoking."
- Tri-tip has a third way to cook: "Smoke, then sear on the smoker". It needs no griddle. The smoker climbs
  to the sear temp while the tri-tip rests off the heat. It is offered only if your smoker reaches 450F.
  If it does not, the choice is greyed out and says "Needs 450F". With no griddle, Add food picks it for you.
- An oven hold never goes below 140F, the safe minimum. The Oven sheet will not step lower.
- Add food opens the food's details first. It goes on the menu when you tap Add to plan.
- The Conflicts fixes are: move to griddle, change temp, or edit the food. "Finish earlier" is gone.
- Warnings read right for foods in the plural, for example "Chicken breasts need a griddle."
- In Celsius, smoker temps are offered in 2C steps.
- If sharing a plan fails, the app says "Couldn't share".
- Open a shared plan. Open a `.pitplan` file from Gmail, Drive or Files and Pit Planner opens on Plan with
  the Open shared plan sheet. It shows the sender's menu. Pick the day under "Serve on". If the sender's
  time has passed or is less than an hour away, the app moves the day and says so ("That time has passed.
  Set for Mon, Nov 23."). Times are worked out for your cookers and your cook rates, not the sender's.
  Tap Use this plan to replace the plan you have now. If the plan then has conflicts, the Conflicts sheet
  opens.
- When you lack a cooker the plan uses, the sheet says what the food will do instead. With no griddle,
  tri-tip reads "Tri-tip will be seared on the smoker, or add a griddle in Gear." If your smoker cannot
  reach the sear temp, it reads "Tri-tip will cook brisket-style with no sear, or add a griddle in Gear."
  If the sender sears on the smoker and you have a griddle, it reads "Tri-tip will be seared on the
  griddle."
- A file that cannot be opened says why: "That file is too big to be a Pit Planner plan." or "Could not read
  that file."

Cook

- Only Start cook on Plan starts a cook. With no cook running, the Cook tab says "No cook running". While
  a cook is running, Start cook is greyed out, so a running cook is never replaced by accident.
- Start over asks first ("Start over?") and never touches your cook history.
- Next up reads as one sentence with the time left, for example "Check the brisket in 1 hr 12 min".
- A step marked Done by mistake can be taken back. In Cook options, the first row is "Undo", followed by the
  step, for example "Undo: Brisket off the smoker". It covers the most recent Done only. If you changed
  something after it, the row says it takes those changes back too.
- If you mark Serve as Done by mistake, Cook finished has a Back to the cook button. It takes the Done back.
- Cook finished has a rate card. If a food finished early or late, it says "Brisket finished early" and
  "Next time the plan uses 0.73 hr/lb instead of 0.75.", with Use 0.73 and Keep 0.75. Keep saves the cook
  but leaves that food out of your rates.
- A warning banner on Live cook. When a change causes a smoker conflict the plan did not have, or makes a
  hold longer than the food should wait, an amber banner says what changed, for example "Smoker conflict:
  brisket at 225F and chicken wings at 400F at the same time." or "Long hold: brisket 6 hr 10 min (limit
  4 hr, keep above 140F)." It does not ring. Tap OK to hide it. The lane chart note names a conflict too.
- Serve moving for one food moves the others. When a Done, +15 min or Back up to temp moves serve, the
  other foods move with it, and Live cook says "Serve moved to 5:15 PM. The other foods moved with it."
  When you mark a step Done early and choose Move serve, serve moves earlier only as far as every other food
  can still be ready.
- The alert on the lock screen has two buttons, Done and +15 min. They work with the app closed.
- The Serve alert says "Time to serve" and lists the menu. The wings pull step reads "Pull wings at
  175-185F", with the safe minimum on the line under it.
- In Cook options, the shift buttons are Earlier and Later.
- If the phone refuses to set the cook's alarms, the Cook screen says "Alarms could not be set. Steps will
  not ring." with Try again.
- Quiet alerts for 30 min now also holds back the repeat alerts.
- A cook in progress survives a font size change and the phone closing the app in the background.
- If the phone restarts during a cook, the alarms come back by themselves. Unlock the phone once after a
  restart: on some phones (seen on a OnePlus) no alert sounds until the first unlock.
- Alarms after a restart were only tested on a OnePlus with Android 16. If you've got a different phone and
  an alarm doesn't ring after a restart, let me know.
- Brine options don't change the plan. They'll be removed in the next update.

Gear

- Gear: you can now pick the temp steps your smoker uses: 5F, 10F, 15F, 25F, or Other.
- Export my data makes one file with your cookers, cook rates and past cooks, and opens the Android share
  sheet. If it fails, the app says "Couldn't export".
- Restore from a file opens your phone's file picker. The app checks the file and asks "Replace your data?",
  and tells you what the file holds. Replace swaps your cookers, rates and cooks for the file's. Your plan
  stays. You cannot restore while a cook is running. If something is wrong with the file, the app says why
  and ends with "Nothing was changed."
- The app checks GitHub for a newer version when you open it, at most once a day. When there is one, Gear
  shows Update available. If you have a beta, the app also offers newer betas and the final release. If you
  have the final release, it offers final releases only. If the check fails, it tries again the next time
  you open the app.
- The Alerts sheet has "Send a test alert in 10 sec", so you can check alerts any time.
- Cook history: a past cook can be edited or removed, and left out of your cook rates.
- Send to developer. If something goes wrong, tap the version line at the bottom of Gear five times. The
  Alarm tests screen opens. Tap Send and pick your email app. The file shows what happened. It holds no
  location, accounts or contacts, and nothing is sent until you tap Send. The address is
  pitplanapp@gmail.com. Tap it to copy it.

## Known limits

- If you tap Done or +15 min on the lock screen while the app is closed, the other foods' alarms keep their
  old times until you open the app. Then they move.
- The plan can ask for wings at 400F on a smoker that cannot reach 400F. Plan does not grey the method out.
- In Celsius, a smoker temp step of 1C to 4C typed by hand is stored off the smoker's grid. If you use
  Celsius, pick a listed step.
- Restore from a file works from Gear only, not from Setup, and not by opening the file from another app.
- Sharing a plan between two phones hasn't been tested on two real phones yet. If something looks off, let
  me know.
